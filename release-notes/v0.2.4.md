## What's new

- Hotword lists per character: correctly written names are never changed, and a misspelling shared by several characters is replaced with the current character's name.
- English misspellings are replaced only where they cannot belong to an ordinary sentence: at the start before a pause, at the end, or on their own.
- The hotword list can be reloaded and the character switched without restarting: `POST /v1/hotwords/reload?path=<file>&role=<id>`.

## Changed

- `PUT /v1/hotwords` and the `~~~` blacklist are removed.
